## Objective

Prove that duration, call, token, cost, concurrency, and rate decisions use one
immutable policy version and produce deterministic below/at/above-boundary
outcomes without protected side effects after denial.

## Preconditions

- The unchanged CAP-02 staging candidate is identified by `CAPABILITY_CANDIDATE_SHA`.
- `FX-PRC-01-CAP02-POLICIES` is applied with `interactive-default@4`.
- The fixture receipt resolves the authorised local `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE`; the
  probe cannot contact external providers or create product-side effects.
- A fresh browser profile resolves `STAGING_SIGN_IN_URL` and protected parameter
  `STAGING_ADMIN_PASSWORD`.

## Procedure

1. **Action (Logged-out visitor):** Open `STAGING_SIGN_IN_URL` in the fresh browser profile.
   **Test data:** `STAGING_SIGN_IN_URL`
   **Expected result:** The page title is `Sign in`.
2. **Action (Logged-out visitor):** Enter the Administrator username in `Username`.
   **Test data:** `admin.alpha`
   **Expected result:** `Username` contains exactly `admin.alpha`.
3. **Action (Logged-out visitor):** Enter the protected password in `Password`.
   **Test data:** protected parameter `STAGING_ADMIN_PASSWORD`
   **Expected result:** `Password` displays only masked characters.
4. **Action (Logged-out visitor):** Select `Sign in`.
   **Test data:** visible action `Sign in`
   **Expected result:** The page title is `Project overview`.
5. **Action (Administrator):** Select project `Atlas Research`.
   **Test data:** project key `ATLAS`
   **Expected result:** `Atlas Research` is displayed as the current project.
6. **Action (Administrator):** Select navigation item `Limits & Policies`.
   **Test data:** visible navigation label `Limits & Policies`
   **Expected result:** The page title is `Limits and policies`.
7. **Action (Administrator):** Open policy version `interactive-default@4`.
   **Test data:** policy `interactive-default@4`
   **Expected result:** The six limit fields display explicit units and `Maximum cost` displays currency `EUR`.
8. **Action (Administrator):** Submit the prepared below-boundary call decision.
   **Test data:** `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE` with maximum `10` and observed `9`
   **Expected result:** The decision is `ALLOW` for policy version `4`.
9. **Action (Administrator):** Submit the prepared exact-boundary call decision.
   **Test data:** `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE` with maximum `10` and observed `10`
   **Expected result:** The decision is `ALLOW` for policy version `4`.
10. **Action (Administrator):** Submit the prepared above-boundary call decision.
    **Test data:** `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE` with maximum `10` and observed `11`
    **Expected result:** The decision is `DENY` and the protected action count remains unchanged.
11. **Action (Administrator):** Submit the prepared over-duration decision.
    **Test data:** `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE` with maximum `300 seconds` and observed `301 seconds`
    **Expected result:** The decision is `DENY` for `Maximum duration`.
12. **Action (Administrator):** Submit the prepared over-token decision.
    **Test data:** `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE` with maximum `20000 tokens` and observed `20001 tokens`
    **Expected result:** The decision is `DENY` for `Maximum tokens`.
13. **Action (Administrator):** Submit the prepared over-cost decision.
    **Test data:** `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE` with maximum `1.50 EUR` and observed `1.51 EUR`
    **Expected result:** The decision is `DENY` for `Maximum cost`.
14. **Action (Administrator):** Submit the prepared over-concurrency decision.
    **Test data:** `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE` with maximum `2` and observed `3`
    **Expected result:** The decision is `DENY` for `Maximum concurrency`.
15. **Action (Administrator):** Submit the prepared over-rate decision.
    **Test data:** `CAP02_LIMIT_PROBE` with maximum `30 requests/60 seconds` and observed `31 requests/60 seconds`
    **Expected result:** The decision is `DENY` for `Rate limit`.
16. **Observation (Administrator):** Inspect the six denial audit events.
    **Test data:** correlation IDs returned by steps 10 through 15
    **Expected result:** Each event records actor, project, exact policy version, boundary, result, UTC timestamp, and correlation ID without secret or provider data.
