## Objective

Prove that invalid credentials produce one actionable generic error without
revealing whether the submitted username exists.

## Preconditions

- The isolated local staging candidate is identified by
  `CAPABILITY_CANDIDATE_SHA`.
- `FX-PRC-01-ADMIN-NO-PROJECT` is prepared and verified.
- The browser has a fresh profile with no Apistra cookies or storage.
- `STAGING_SIGN_IN_URL` is available.

## Procedure

1. **Action (Logged-out visitor):** Open `STAGING_SIGN_IN_URL` in the fresh browser profile.
   **Test data:** `STAGING_SIGN_IN_URL`
   **Expected result:** DSN-001 has page title `Sign in` and renders `Username`, `Password`, and `Sign in`.
2. **Action (Logged-out visitor):** Enter an unknown synthetic username.
   **Test data:** `missing.user`
   **Expected result:** `Username` contains exactly `missing.user`.
3. **Action (Logged-out visitor):** Enter the synthetic invalid password.
   **Test data:** `Invalid-Only-For-Test-01!`
   **Expected result:** `Password` displays only masked characters.
4. **Action (Logged-out visitor):** Select `Sign in` once.
   **Test data:** visible action `Sign in`
   **Expected result:** The inline alert reads `Sign-in failed. Check your credentials and try again.`.
5. **Observation (Logged-out visitor):** Inspect the credential fields after the failed submission.
   **Test data:** fields `Username` and `Password`
   **Expected result:** `Username` retains `missing.user` and `Password` is empty.
6. **Observation (Logged-out visitor):** Inspect the current page and focus target.
   **Test data:** sign-in error alert
   **Expected result:** Focus is on the error alert, the page remains `Sign in`, and no account-existence, provider, stack, or infrastructure detail is rendered.

## Cleanup

Close the browser profile and restore `FX-PRC-01-ADMIN-NO-PROJECT`. The case
must not create or revoke a session.

## Required evidence and oracle

  start/end, and attempt;
- pass only if the exact generic text, field reset, focus, and non-disclosure
  expectations all match.
